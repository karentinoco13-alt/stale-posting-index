Is this company or job real?

Search a company name, its website, or paste a job link someone sent you. We check the company's own website and official job listings and flag anything that doesn't add up. Results are saved, so everyone who searches this company sees the warnings.

Job posts open too long

Every company is held to the same standard, whatever its size. When a role has been posted for more than days, we draft a friendly note asking the company to take it down if it's filled, or refresh it if they're still looking. Notes open in your own email app; nothing is sent automatically.

        Staying safe

        • Real employers never ask you to pay for training, equipment or "processing".
        • Apply only through the company's own link. Every job here links to one.
        • Be wary of interviews held only over chat apps, or offers made without an interview.
