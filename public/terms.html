What this app is

Real Jobs Finder collects job openings from employers' own websites and official job boards, runs automated checks for signs that a company or job may not be genuine, and shows candidates' ratings of employers' recruiting teams. It's a free, non-commercial tool. It is not a recruiter, an employer, or a job board, and it does not handle applications.

No guarantees

  • Listings come from the employer and can change or close at any time. Always confirm details on the employer's own site before applying.
  • Flags such as "Be careful" and "Warning: may not be real" come from automated checks (for example a new domain, or a job that isn't on the company's listings). They are signals to look more closely, not a finding that a company has done anything wrong. A real company can be flagged by mistake, and a fake one can be missed.
  • Recruiting ratings and comments are the personal opinions of the candidates who wrote them.
  • The app is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind.

Your responsibilities

  • Rate only companies whose recruiting process you actually took part in, and rate each company once.
  • Keep comments honest, factual and respectful. No names, personal details, threats, harassment, or false statements.
  • If you send a note to an employer, keep it courteous. The app drafts notes for you to review; you are the sender.
  • Don't use the app to scrape, overload or attack any website.

Never pay to get a job

Genuine employers don't ask candidates to pay for training, equipment, background checks or "processing", and don't hire through chat apps alone. If that happens, stop and report it to your local consumer-protection or fraud authority.

For employers

If you think your company was flagged by mistake, or you've updated or closed a posting, use Send feedback and we'll review it. Once old postings are closed on your official job board, the flags clear automatically on the next refresh.
