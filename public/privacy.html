Privacy and security

Last updated November 3, 2026

Real Jobs Finder is built to collect as little as possible. There are no accounts, no advertising and no tracking cookies.

Visits are counted with GoatCounter, which uses no cookies and does not collect personal data.

What we store

  • Recruiting ratings: your star rating, the optional answers (did they reply, were you ghosted, were you respected, how far you got), your optional comment, and the date. Your comment and rating are shown to other users without your name.
  • An anonymous browser ID: a random code kept in your browser so that you can rate each company once, update your rating, and delete your data. It isn't linked to your name, email or device.
  • Feedback: what you write in the feedback form, plus your email only if you choose to give one. Feedback is delivered privately to the app's developer and stored in the developer's Google account, along with the page address and the time it was sent.
  • Company checks: the company or job link that was checked and the result. These aren't linked to you.
  • Employer notes: when someone marks a note to a company as sent, the date and the company's public hiring email.

What we don't do

  • We never send email for you. Notes to employers open in your email app, and you choose whether to send them.
  • We don't sell or share your data, and we don't ask for your name, CV or phone number.
  • Job applications happen on the employer's own website, under that employer's privacy policy, not ours.

Please don't share personal details

Keep rating comments about the hiring process. Don't include names of recruiters or other people, phone numbers, or anything that identifies you.

Security

  • When the app checks a company, it reads only public web pages and public job-board data.
  • It refuses to scan private or internal network addresses, limits how much it downloads, and times out slow sites.
  • Ratings, checks and feedback are rate limited to slow down spam and fake reviews.
  • Everything users type is escaped before display, so it can't run code in your browser.

Delete your data

Use Delete all my data at the bottom of any page. It permanently removes your ratings, comments and feedback, and clears the app's storage in your browser. To delete feedback already delivered to the developer, ask through Send feedback.
